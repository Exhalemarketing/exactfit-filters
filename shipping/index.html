Shipping (US & Canada)

Every order ships directly from our supplier to you. US orders ship from US warehouses; Canadian orders are priced in CAD and ship from a supplier in Canada, so you don't pay cross-border duties.

  • United States: priced in USD. Shipping is included in the price for the 48 contiguous states; Alaska, Hawaii and territories: ask us first.
  • Canada: priced in CAD, shipping included. All provinces; remote and northern addresses may take longer.
  • We include the supplier's delivery estimate with your payment link and email tracking once your order ships. The box may show the supplier's name.
