Questions

What is the actual size of a 20x20x1 air filter?

A 20x20x1 filter measures about 19.5 x 19.5 x 0.75 inches. 20x20x1 is the nominal (rounded) size printed on the frame; the real filter is slightly smaller so it slides into the slot.

Is MERV 11 right for my home?

MERV 11 is a step up for homes with pets or allergies: it also catches pet dander and finer dust. Most residential systems handle it well.

How often should I change it?

Most 1-inch filters should be changed every 1-3 months: monthly with pets, allergies or heavy use; up to 90 days in light use. A 6-pack usually covers 6-12 months.
