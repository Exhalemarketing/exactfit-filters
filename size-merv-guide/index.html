Air filter size and MERV guide

Your filter size is printed on the frame of the filter you have now (for example 16x25x1). That is the nominal size; the actual filter is about half an inch smaller in length and width, and 3/4 inch thick.

Which MERV?

  • MERV 8 — dust, lint, pollen. The standard for most homes.
  • MERV 11 — adds pet dander and finer dust. Good for pets and allergies.
  • MERV 13 — finest capture, including smoke. Confirm your system supports it (see your furnace or AC manual).

No size printed?

Measure length, width and thickness of the old filter, then round each number up to the nearest whole inch to get the nominal size.
