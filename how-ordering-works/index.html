How ordering works

  1. Send an order request from any product page. Nothing is charged on the site.
  2. We confirm the live price and stock with our supplier and email you a secure payment link (USD for the US, CAD for Canada).
  3. After you pay, we place the order and the filters ship directly from the supplier to your address.
  4. We email your tracking number as soon as the supplier provides it.

If a size is out of stock or the price changes, we tell you before charging anything.

ExactFit Filters is an independent online retailer. Filters ship directly from our supplier partners in the US and Canada. Product images marked "Representative image" are generic illustrations, not photos of a specific brand. Brand names belong to their owners.
